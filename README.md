# Webitoo AR Lessons

Web AR experiments for school students, made by Webitoo. One folder per lesson (`/<lesson>/index.html` + `.usdz`), served with GitHub Pages.

- `human-skeleton/` — Human skeleton, 206 bones. Skeleton model: "Male Skeleton" by projectkaizen (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/male-skeleton-11b57ebfcf6c4e3b88d0cbe618ee70a7), CC BY 4.0 — posed, split into regions and ear ossicles added by Webitoo.
- `daniell-cell/` — Daniell cell and lithium-ion cell (electrons, Zn²⁺/Cu²⁺, salt bridge, Li⁺). Models built in Blender by Webitoo.
- `ammeter/` — The ammeter: a galvanometer turned into an ammeter with a shunt, connected in series, and its ranges.
- `voltmeter/` — The voltmeter: a galvanometer turned into a voltmeter with a multiplier, connected in parallel, and its ranges.
- `ohmmeter/` — The ohmmeter: battery, rheostat and galvanometer, zero adjustment, and measuring an unknown resistance.
- `kirchhoff/` — Kirchhoff's laws: two batteries, three resistors, the junction rule and the loop rule.
- `magnetic-flux/` — Magnetic flux: Φ = B A cos θ as a loop turns in a uniform field.
- `magnetic-force/` — Magnetic force on a wire: F = B I L sin θ and Fleming's left-hand rule.
- `magnetic-torque/` — Magnetic torque on a coil: the couple on a current loop, τ = B I A N cos θ.
- `straight-wire-field/` — Field around a straight wire: iron filings, compasses and the right-hand grip rule.
- `two-wires/` — Force between two parallel wires: attraction, repulsion and the neutral point.
- `circular-coil/` — Field of a circular coil: the field shape, N and S faces, and B ∝ N I / r.
- `solenoid/` — Field of a solenoid: inside vs outside, the poles, and the iron core.

The physics labs open with **View in AR** (Android: the interactive lab on your table; iPhone: AR Quick Look with a still 3D model, animated for magnetic flux and magnetic torque). Each has a QR-free `card.png` for the website card.
